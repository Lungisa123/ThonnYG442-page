A continuation of the saga

In the booth, Cape Town

Kamvalethu Tony Gumbe — known as Tony G442, AKA The Special Kid On Earth — is a diverse sound artist, stylist innovator, designer, model and peer educator with a forever-present, God-driven motion.

Hailing from East London, Tony G made his name in the Cape Town hip-hop scene after releasing his first anticipated project, Sub Level Season EP, and has since worked with brands inside and outside the Western Cape. In 2024 he released his first commercial project, Special Kids On Earth.

Special Kids On Earth 2 carries the saga forward. Across the collection, Tony G leans into self-belief, overcoming adversity and the weight of individuality — introspective themes delivered through his lyrical prowess, diverse sound and unique flow.
